# Code of Conduct

This is a small, friendly, volunteer project for DJs and producers. To keep it that way:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.
- Keep to the topic. Help requests about this device are welcome; requests for keys, pirated software or music, or ways around licences or protections are not, and will be closed.
- Do not share other people's private information, and never post your own database key, library files or settings.
- Be patient. Maintainers are volunteers and may take a while to reply.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use the repository's private **Report a vulnerability** form under the **Security** tab and say it is a conduct matter, or contact the maintainer through the profile linked from the repository.
